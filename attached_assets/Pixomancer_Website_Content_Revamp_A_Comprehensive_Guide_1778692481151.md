**1\. Introduction and Overview**

This document outlines a complete content revamp for the Pixomancer website, aiming to enhance its search engine optimization (SEO) and answer engine optimization (AEO) friendliness, improve readability, and strengthen its conversion-oriented messaging. **2\. General Content Guidelines**

* **Tone of Voice:** Confident, authoritative, innovative, results-driven, and approachable. We aim for a professional yet engaging tone that resonates with visionary brands and decision-makers.

* **Language:** American English, easy to understand, avoiding jargon where possible, or explaining it clearly when necessary.

* **SEO/AEO Focus:** Content will be rich in relevant keywords and phrases, structured to answer common user queries, and designed for optimal visibility in search results and AI-powered answer engines. Keywords will be naturally integrated, focusing on user intent.

* **Conversational Copy:** Use powerful, action-oriented words that drive engagement and encourage conversion. Emphasize benefits over features.

* **Structure:** Each page's content will be broken down into logical sections with clear headings and subheadings, making it easy for developers to implement and for users to digest.
